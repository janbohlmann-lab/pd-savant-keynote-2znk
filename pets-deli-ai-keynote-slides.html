Five things we would tell any CEO.

1

Build the knowledge first.

We started with the brain, not the tools. Every machine since then plugs into it.

2

Put everything on one network.

A machine that cannot call the others is just another silo — with a nicer interface.

3

Rent the models. Own the system.

Models change every few weeks. Your data, your rules and your wiring do not.

4

Keep a human on the slider.

Machines make the volume. People make the call — on every ad, every reply, every claim.

5

Give every project a number.

Every AI project gets an owner, a number and a done-when — or it does not get built.
